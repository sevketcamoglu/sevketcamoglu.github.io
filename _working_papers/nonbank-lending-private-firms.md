abstract: >-
  Using data from the U.S. syndicated loan market, we document the importance of nonbank credit for
  private firms. Both aggregate and micro-level tests show that nonbank lending and lending to private
  firms are more cyclical than bank lending and lending to public firms, respectively. The evidence
  suggests the growth of nonbank financial intermediation and the rising importance of private firms
  are tightly interlinked phenomena, raising questions about the potential of these segments to amplify
  the financial cycle.
